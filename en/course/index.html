ProGeriate

Fashion design fundamentals, taught in Procreate

Why this course?

“This course is not only about learning the tools. It is a path towards seeing your ideas more clearly, giving them shape, and turning them into a finished design. From designing garments and understanding how fabrics behave, to building a mood board and working with digital tools — everything sits alongside everything else, so that you can design with a more professional eye and a visual language of your own. You are not here only to learn technique; you are here to learn how to design.”

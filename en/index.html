About Me

ROJAN SABOUNI

I’m Rojan Sabouni, 18 years old, and currently studying Fashion and Fashion Design at university.

I studied Fashion Design at a vocational high school for three years and am now continuing my studies at university. I have skills in sewing, pattern making, garment construction, and fashion design, both by hand and digitally using Procreate.

Alongside fashion design, I have received training in Artificial Intelligence, have practical experience working with AI, and hold an international certification in the field. I’m also interested in creatively combining fashion design and AI to develop new ideas and approaches to design.

My goal is to create fresh ideas in fashion and transform them into work that reflects my own identity and creative perspective.

Personal Design

The Design Process

Step 1 / 7

Getting to know you

We begin with a conversation: your taste, your lifestyle, the occasion, and how you want to feel wearing the piece.

Step 2 / 7

Finding the idea

A few directions take shape from that conversation, and together we choose the one that feels most like you.

Step 3 / 7

Building the mood board

I gather images, textures and colours into a mood board, so the feeling of the design is clear before the first line is drawn.

Step 4 / 7

Reading the body silhouette

I study your silhouette so that cuts, lines and proportions are chosen to sit on you at their best.

Step 5 / 7

Designing the details

Neckline, sleeves, seams and embellishment are designed one by one — the details are what make a piece truly yours.

Step 6 / 7

Choosing colour and material

We choose colour and fabric together, around your skin tone, the season and the mood the design should carry.

Step 7 / 7

The final design

Everything comes together in one finished design, ready to be made.
